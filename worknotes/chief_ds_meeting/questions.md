## To be covered in a separate session: the downstream lifecycle, from a blank slate

> **Separate session.** We want the CDS to come up with ideas from a blank slate here. The current
> stages (register → promote → deploy) and the questions below are our starting points, not
> constraints. We may end up with something quite different.

| Topic | Starting questions |
|---|---|
| **Lifecycle design** | s1. The lifecycle steps today: **register** (local → registered), **promote** (choose the version for production; the approved PR is the sign-off), **deploy** (the change-request process copies it to production). A data scientist's session never writes to production. Is this the right shape, and what should "registered" mean: any run someone wants to keep, or a gated candidate for production? <sub>D-033, OD-09</sub><br>s2. Versions: how many per model, exactly one in production at a time, and do we want shadow challengers (a new version scoring next to production before it takes over)? <sub>OD-07</sub><br>s3. Registry storage: our own append-only records with tables on top (shaped like MLflow's, so a later move is a mapping), or MLflow itself? <sub>OD-08</sub><br>s4. Deployment copies the model to production paths, rather than pointing production at sandpit paths. Is that right? <sub>lifecycle.md</sub> |
